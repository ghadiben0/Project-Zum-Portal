Resource Planning 
===================================

Use **Resource Planning** to plan and assign **Employees** to **Forecast Projects** and **Current Projects** .

.. code-block:: console

   Please take into consideration that Each Employee can be assigned to multiple **Forecast Projects** and **Current Projects** per month.

Forecast Allocation
_____________________________________

**Fields Definition**

  *Normal Hours*
________

  Normal Hours is the total working hours for the allocated Month

  *Billable Hours*
__________

The total Billable Hours triggered from **Allocate Resources** screen.

  *Non-Billable Hours*
__________
The total Non-Billable Hours triggered from **Allocate Resources** screen.

  *Allocation Percentage*
__________
Allocation%  is based on this formula:
         Forecasted Billable Hours+Non-Billable Hours/Normal Hours for the allocated Month*100
